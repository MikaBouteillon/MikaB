MiniMundo · Request for ProposalThe concept
02 · The concept

Not a playground. A city.

Small streets. Themed buildings. A shopfront on every façade. Children walk through freely and discover trades, shops and everyday life. Our positioning is premium.

A visitor must feel they are walking down a real street, not past a row of separate boxes.
A

Streets, not modules

Façades and interiors are the experience. The architecture must be realistic and rich in detail.

B

Four kinds of play

Roleplay. Interactive and educational games. Physical soft-play built into the buildings. Free play with no fixed route.

C

Detail everywhere

Shopfronts, signage, mouldings, shutters, planters, lighting, street furniture, greenery. Colourful, playful, warm.

D

Not a copy

We do not want an existing concept repeated. We want a freer, more open experience.

MiniMundo · Request for ProposalThe action zone
03 · The action zone

One more world: the action zone.

Next to the streets of the miniature city we want one big action zone. A different world: louder, more physical, built for the wow effect and for high capacity. It is what lets us welcome many children at the same time.

01

Illuminated slides

Slides that light up. Colour, movement and light pull children in from across the room.

02

Construction and excavation

A dig site with ride-on and hand-operated diggers. Children move real material, not just look at machines.

03

Ball pit park

A large ball pool with games built into it: cannons, targets, tunnels, climbing. Not an empty pool of balls.

04

Ninja course

A short obstacle course of the kind children already know: hanging steps, ropes, balance beams, a soft landing.

These four can sit together in one big zone, or be split across the floor. Tell us what works best, and how many children each element handles at once.

07 · Technical specifications

Architecture, play and materials.

Façade detail

  • Open or semi-open shopfronts. Visitors must see inside each space.
  • Doors and windows must be real architectural openings, not flat cut-outs.
  • A different sign for every building, backlit or illuminated where possible.
  • Mouldings, shutters, planters and street furniture: lampposts, benches, street signs.
  • Different materials and textures from one building to the next.

Types of play

  • Roleplay. Children act out everyday situations.
  • Interactive and educational games. Things to handle and try.
  • Physical and soft-play built directly into the buildings and façades.
  • Free play. No fixed route through the park.
  • The action zone of section 03: slides, dig site, ball pit and ninja course, sized for high capacity.
  • The whole venue must read as one street, with landmark buildings, smaller lanes, and a central square where it makes sense.

Materials & maintenance

  • Any material is accepted: timber, composite, metal or other. It must reach the quality shown in the references.
  • Built for heavy daily use in a supervised children's venue.
  • Easy to clean and maintain every day.
  • Finishes must match the quality level of the reference images.
  • Name the exact material used for every module.

07 · Technical specifications

Safety, regulations and certification.

Child safety

  • Young children will use this park every day. Plan for it from the first drawing.
  • Play surfaces, walls and edges within a child's reach must be soft and shock-absorbing.
  • Rounded corners and edges everywhere. No sharp parts at child height.
  • Non-slip flooring that is easy to disinfect, in every play area.
  • All materials non-toxic and compliant with children's safety standards.

French ERP regulations

  • The venue is a French ERP (public venue). The design must work with French rules on accessibility, fire safety and smoke extraction.
  • Our architect will confirm these constraints with you during the detailed costing phase.
  • You are not expected to master French rules now. Tell us you can comply.

Certification

  • Play equipment and furniture must meet the safety standards of the country of installation: France and the European Union.
  • Send us your product certificates with your answer.
  • Confirm in writing that every material used meets current standards.
